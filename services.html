Our Podiatry Services

Expert, compassionate care brought directly to your home. We offer a full range of non-surgical podiatry treatments.

Nail Trimming

Safe and hygienic toenail trimming is essential, especially for seniors or those with limited mobility. We expertly cut and file toenails to prevent ingrown nails, fungal infections, and severe discomfort, ensuring your feet remain healthy and pain-free.

Callus Shavings

Thick, rough calluses and corns can cause significant pain when walking. Our professional reduction and shaving techniques safely remove excess dead skin, providing immediate relief and improved comfort in your daily activities.

Diabetic Foot Evaluation

Diabetes can cause nerve damage and poor circulation in the feet. We provide thorough visual assessments, sensory testing (neuropathy checks), and patient education to catch issues early and prevent serious complications.

Wound Assessments & Care

Proper wound care is critical for healing. We offer professional assessment, treatment, and management of foot ulcers, surgical wounds, and infections, using advanced dressings to promote rapid healing and prevent amputation.
