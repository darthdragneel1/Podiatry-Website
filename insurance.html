Accepted Insurance Plans

We accept a wide variety of Medicare and Medicaid plans to ensure you get the care you need. Contact us to verify your specific coverage.

Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan / Blue Care Network

Highly rated locally, featuring plans like Medicare Plus Blue (PPO) and BCN Advantage (HMO-POS).

Priority Health Medicare

Frequently earns high quality star ratings from CMS for its extensive HMO-POS and PPO plans.

HAP Senior Plus

Henry Ford Health's dedicated Medicare line, widely used in Metro Detroit with $0 premium plan tiers.

Humana

One of the largest national networks footprinted in Michigan, valued for its Part B premium giveback options.

Aetna Medicare

Offers highly rated national networks with extensive PPO structures across the state.

Wellcare (by Meridian)

Centene's Medicare arm, focusing heavily on standalone Prescription Drug Plans (Part D) and low-premium Advantage options.

Trinity Health Plan of Michigan

A specialized provider-backed Medicare plan linked directly with Trinity Health systems.

Meridian Health Plan of Michigan

Centene’s subsidiary and Michigan’s largest Medicaid HMO plan.

Blue Cross Complete of Michigan

Contracted to provide Medicaid and Healthy Michigan plan options across 58 counties in the Lower Peninsula.

Priority Health Choice

Administers Medicaid, MIChild, and Children’s Special Health Care Services (CSHCS).

UnitedHealthcare Community Plan

Covers a major portion of the state's Medicaid beneficiaries and offers integrated dual-eligible plans.

McLaren Health Plan

Boasts a highly comprehensive network, authorized to serve Medicaid beneficiaries in every county of the Lower Peninsula.

Molina Healthcare of Michigan

A major statewide program focused entirely on government-sponsored care.

HAP Empowered / HAP CareSource

The Medicaid arm of Health Alliance Plan, predominantly focused on Southeast and East-Central Michigan regions.

Aetna Better Health of Michigan

Manages comprehensive health care benefits for eligible Medicaid and integrated program enrollees.

Don't see your plan listed? We also accept general Medicare and Medicaid.
